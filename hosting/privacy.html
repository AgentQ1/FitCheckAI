This Privacy Policy explains what information FitCheck AI ("the app," "we," "us") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you have questions, contact us at moomolab@gmail.com.

How We Use Your Information

Photos you submit may be processed by automated AI systems to generate outfit feedback, ratings, and styling suggestions. You choose which photos to upload.

The app includes optional public features such as the Arena and leaderboard. Content (such as your outfit photo and related activity) may be visible to other users only when you choose to participate in public features or make content public. You can avoid public visibility by not opting into these features.

Your Rights
